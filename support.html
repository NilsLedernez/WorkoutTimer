Frequently asked questions

How do I save a workout?

Set up your sets, rounds and timings on the main screen, tap Save Workout As… and give it a name. Open Saved Workouts to load it again later.

How do I edit, reorder or delete a saved workout?

In Saved Workouts, swipe right on a workout to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. Tap Edit in the top corner and drag the handles to change the order.

I can't hear the beeps.

Turn up the volume with the volume buttons and check that your phone isn't connected to a Bluetooth speaker or headphones you've left elsewhere. WorkoutTimer plays its sounds mixed with other audio, so music or podcasts keep playing.

What do the different beeps mean?

You hear a short, low beep once per second during the last three seconds of each phase. When the phase actually changes, a higher-pitched beep plays: a double beep when work starts and a single beep when rest starts.

Does the timer keep running when my screen locks?

Yes. WorkoutTimer keeps running in the background while a workout is active so the beeps continue. While the workout screen is open, the display also won't dim automatically.

Why does the app use background audio?

It's what lets the timer and its beeps continue when you lock your phone or switch apps mid-workout. The app doesn't record anything.

Do I need an account? Is my data backed up?

No account is needed. Saved workouts live only on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, but if you delete the app without a backup, they're gone.
