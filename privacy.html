WorkoutTimer Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short: WorkoutTimer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

Who is responsible

WorkoutTimer (“the app”) is published by Nils Ledernez (“I”, “me”). You can contact me here.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, health data, photos, identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports or any other personal information. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics software, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

If you choose to save a workout, its name and settings (number of sets and rounds, and work and rest durations) are stored locally on your device using the standard iOS storage for app settings. This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your own iPhone or iPad backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are controlled by you and Apple under your device settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions and device features

  • Audio: the app plays beeps through your device’s speaker or headphones. It continues to play audio in the background so the timer keeps working while your screen is locked. The app never records audio and does not access your microphone.
  • Screen: the app asks iOS to keep the screen awake while a workout is running.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices ever change, I will update this page and the “last updated” date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us or visit the support page.
