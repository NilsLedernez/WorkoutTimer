WorkoutTimer

A simple, big-number interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, boxing and circuit training. Set it up once, hit Start, and focus on the workout.

Features

  • Sets & roundsChoose how many sets and rounds per set you want to do.
  • Work & rest timingSet work, rest between rounds, rest between sets and a prep countdown with an iOS-style minutes and seconds wheel.
  • Saved workoutsName and save your routines, then edit, reorder or delete them any time.
  • Clear audio cuesShort beeps in the last three seconds of every phase, and a higher-pitched beep when work and rest switch.
  • Huge, readable displaySee the time, round and phase from across the room.
  • Screen stays onThe display won't dim mid-workout.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your workouts are stored only on your device. Read the privacy policy.
